How does this work?

The banana cube is a 2×2×3 puzzle with a banana shape. The stem end (U), the middle (E) and the tip end (D) each turn by quarter turns. The long sides (R L F B) only turn by half turns.

When the page opens, it checks every possible position with a breadth-first search: 241,920 of them, or 1,935,360 if you also count which way you hold it. That makes the move count exact. It is the fewest moves possible, so it is not a guess.

In the tree, each row is a distance from solved, and the shaded band shows how many positions are at that distance. The yellow branch is your shortest way home. The coloured dots are all the moves you can make from here. Click one to try it.
