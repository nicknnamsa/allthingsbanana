Rhythm The engine room

Tempo
Tap along to a track to match its speed
Kick hardness
0 = clean house thump, 10 = hard-dance punch (click, deep pitch drop, longer tail). Stays clean: no fizz.
Bounce
How hard the off-beat bass pushes back against the kick, and how deep the pump is.

Synths The serious production

Lead brightness
The buzzy detuned saw lead that doubles the squeak melody.
Layering
Auto: squeak alone in the intro and build, + lead in breakdowns, everything in drops.
Bass grit
Distortion on the bass's mid-range. The sub underneath stays clean, so it's gritty but still deep.

Structure Where the tension comes from

Build length
Snare roll and riser before the drop. Short = punchy.
Drop contrast
How empty the bar before the drop is. 10 = total silence, then everything at once.
Breakdown depth
How far it strips back between drops. Around 4–5 never gets properly quiet.

Vibe Absurd source, serious production

Silliness
How loud and squeaky the rubber-banana melody is. The production stays deadly serious either way.
Chop rate
How fast the melody is chopped. The banana's spin is locked to it: half a turn per chop.
